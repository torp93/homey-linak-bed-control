Your adjustable bed already knows how to raise your head and lift your legs.
This app lets Homey do it too, so the bed can follow the rest of your home
instead of waiting for someone to find the remote. Reading in bed, getting up
in the morning, turning off the light underneath when the house goes to sleep
— all of it can happen on its own.

Everything runs on your own network, over Bluetooth through an ESP32 you place
near the bed. Nothing is sent to a manufacturer, no account is needed, and the
bed's own remote keeps working as before. Developed for LINAK TD4 and TD5
control boxes, tested on Svane Zefir beds.
